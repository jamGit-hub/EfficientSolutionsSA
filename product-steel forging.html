How It's Made

From Raw Billet to Inspected Forging

An eight-stage, quality-controlled workflow — every forging is tracked from raw material through final non-destructive testing before release.

01

Raw Material

Certified steel billet selected to the specified grade.

02

Cutting

Billet sized and cut to the required forging weight.

03

Heating

Controlled furnace heating to optimal forging temperature.

04

Forging

Press-forged to shape, refining grain flow and strength.

05

Normalizing

Heat treatment to relieve stress and stabilize the grain structure.

06

Machining

Rough or finish machining to drawing dimensions.

07

QT Heat Treatment

Quenching and tempering to reach target mechanical properties.

08

Inspection (UT, MT)

Ultrasonic and magnetic particle testing before release.

Typical lead times vary by delivery condition: forged & normalized from as little as 7–10 days, rough-machined from 10–15 days, and rough-machined with quenching & tempering from 15–20 days. Special processes are quoted on request.
