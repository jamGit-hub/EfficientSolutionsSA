Explosion Protection

Explosion Protection Systems

Venting, isolation, detection and suppression for facilities that handle combustible dust — certified to CE, ATEX, IECEx and UL.

Flameless Explosion Venting

Safely relieves dust-explosion pressure indoors while keeping flame and burning dust inside the device.

  • ATEX-certified indoor venting
  • Protects staff & nearby equipment

Explosion Venting Panel

Passive rupture panels that burst at a preset pressure to release deflagration pressure and flame.

  • No moving parts, low maintenance
  • For collectors, silos & vessels

Explosion Isolation Valve

Pinch and flap-type valves that stop an explosion from travelling through ducts to connected equipment.

  • Passive and active versions
  • Active valves link to plant controls

Spark Detection & Extinguishing

Detects sparks travelling in ducts and extinguishes them automatically before they reach the collector.

  • Duct-mounted spark detectors
  • Automatic extinguishing & control cabinet

Explosion Suppression System

Detects an explosion at its earliest stage and injects suppressant to stop it inside the protected vessel.

  • Suppressant container & control unit
  • Touchscreen monitoring

Inert Powder Feeder

Feeds inert powder into the dust stream to raise minimum ignition energy (MIE) and lower explosion risk.

  • VL-PFQ Series
  • Laser notching & tab welding lines

How We Work

From Dust Analysis to Commissioning

Every system is engineered around the actual dust in your plant — tested, simulated and designed before equipment is selected.

01

On-Site Inspection

Survey of dust sources, existing collection, ventilation and explosion risks.

02

Dust Sampling

Representative samples collected from your process.

03

Explosibility Testing

Composition and explosibility of the dust measured.

04

Equipment Selection

Collectors, vacuums and protection devices matched to the hazard.

05

Airflow Simulation

Fluid simulation to confirm capture velocity and airflow.

06

Duct Design

Ductwork routed and sized for efficient, safe extraction.

07

Installation

Installation and commissioning by trained technicians.

08

Safety training, scheduled maintenance and spare parts.
