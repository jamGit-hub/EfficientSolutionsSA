Applications

Where Control Transformers Are Used

From machine tool panels to outdoor substations — built for the full range of industrial control circuit demands.

Machine Tool Control Panels

Powering control circuits in CNC machines, lathes, and milling equipment — providing safe, isolated low-voltage supply to PLC inputs and relay coils.

Industrial Automation Systems

Supplying stable, isolated power to PLC racks, relay-based logic systems, and SCADA interface equipment across automated production lines.

Harsh Environment Installations

Dual-cover sealed designs rated for high humidity, dust ingress, and heavy vibration — ideal for outdoor substations and processing plant environments.

MCC & Control Panel Assemblies

Integrated into motor control centres and custom electrical panels — delivering the 24 V or 120 V control voltage that switchgear and protection relays demand.

Safety Circuit Isolation

Galvanic isolation between high-voltage supply and operator-accessible control circuits — protecting personnel and sensitive instrumentation from fault currents.

Custom Panel Builds

Available in any VA rating, primary/secondary voltage combination, and enclosure class — designed to spec for bespoke panel builders and system integrators.
